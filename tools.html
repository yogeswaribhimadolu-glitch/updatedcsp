How to choose the right tool

Start with the task

Decide what you need first: writing, code, a picture, a video or research. Then pick from that category instead of trying every tool.

Try the free plan

Most tools have a free plan with limits. Test with a real task from your own work before paying for anything.

Check privacy

Read what the tool does with your data. Do not upload private or confidential files to a tool you do not trust.

Compare two tools

Give the same prompt to two tools and compare the results. Keep the one that gives clearer and more accurate answers for you.
