How these terms connect

A quick map of how the pieces fit together, from the model itself to how you use it.

  1. Neural network → TransformerA Transformer is a specific, very successful type of neural network design, built around a mechanism called attention.
  2. Transformer + training data → LLMTraining a Transformer on huge amounts of text produces a Large Language Model. Training determines its parameters.
  3. LLM + a task → InferenceEvery time you send a prompt, the model performs inference: it uses its trained parameters to predict the next tokens.
  4. LLM + your own data → RAG or fine-tuningTo make an LLM answer using your own documents, you either retrieve and attach relevant text at request time (RAG) or retrain part of the model on your data (fine-tuning).
  5. LLM + step-by-step training → Reasoning modelTraining a model to work through problems in steps before answering produces a reasoning model, better at maths and logic.
  6. LLM + other data types → Multimodal modelTraining on images and audio as well as text produces a multimodal model that can understand more than plain text.
